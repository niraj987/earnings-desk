Acme Technologies Q2 FY2026 Earnings Release

Acme Technologies today reported results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $4.55 billion, up 9% year over year.
Gross margin was 43.0%.
Operating margin was 18.9%.
Net income was $598 million, or $2.38 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $610 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $2.3 billion, while total debt stood at $3.4 billion.

Outlook
For the third quarter, the company expects revenue between $4.70 billion and $4.80 billion.
