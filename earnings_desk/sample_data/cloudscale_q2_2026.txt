CloudScale AI Systems Q2 FY2026 Earnings Release

CloudScale AI Systems today reported financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $2.05 billion, up 30% year over year.
Gross margin was 73.0%.
Operating margin was 26.5%.
Net income was $390 million, or $2.60 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $440 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $3.5 billion, while total debt stood at $800 million.

Outlook
For the third quarter, the company expects revenue between $2.30 billion and $2.40 billion.
