NovaRetail Group Q2 FY2026 Earnings Release

NovaRetail Group today reported results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $6.30 billion, up 1% year over year.
Gross margin was 33.0%.
Operating margin was 7.5%.
Net income was $140 million, or $0.62 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $110 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $1.3 billion, while total debt stood at $4.5 billion.

Outlook
For the third quarter, the company expects revenue between $6.10 billion and $6.20 billion.
