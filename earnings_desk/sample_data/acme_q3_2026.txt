Acme Technologies Q3 FY2026 Earnings Release

Acme Technologies today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $4.82 billion, up 12% year over year.
Gross margin was 41.5%.
Operating margin was 18.2%.
Net income was $612 million, or $2.45 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $540 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $2.1 billion, while total debt stood at $3.4 billion.

Risks and Other Items
Supply-chain constraints on key components limited shipments late in the quarter.
Foreign exchange headwinds reduced reported revenue by approximately 1.2 percentage points.
The company is party to ongoing litigation related to a patent dispute, and an adverse ruling could be material.
Two customers accounted for 38% of total revenue.

Outlook
For the fourth quarter, the company expects revenue between $5.00 billion and $5.20 billion and operating margin of approximately 18%.
Management is confident in continued demand momentum and expects a strong holiday quarter.
The outlook assumes continued caution around macroeconomic visibility.
