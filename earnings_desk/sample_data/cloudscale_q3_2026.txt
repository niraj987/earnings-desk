CloudScale AI Systems Q3 FY2026 Earnings Release

CloudScale AI Systems today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $2.40 billion, up 36% year over year.
Gross margin was 74.5%.
Operating margin was 28.0%.
Net income was $480 million, or $3.15 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $520 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $3.8 billion, while total debt stood at $800 million.

Risks and Other Items
The company experienced strong enterprise adoption across global tier-1 hyperscalers.
Foreign exchange headwinds reduced reported revenue by approximately 0.5 percentage points.

Outlook
For the fourth quarter, the company expects revenue between $2.60 billion and $2.75 billion and operating margin of approximately 29%.
Management is confident in accelerating AI enterprise workload demand.
