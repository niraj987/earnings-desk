NovaRetail Group Q3 FY2026 Earnings Release

NovaRetail Group today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Total revenue was $6.10 billion, down 2% year over year.
Gross margin was 31.5%.
Operating margin was 6.2%.
Net income was $95 million, or $0.42 per diluted share.
Free cash flow was $70 million.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $1.1 billion, while total debt stood at $4.6 billion.

Risks and Other Items
Supply-chain constraints on transportation networks increased logistics expenditures significantly.
Foreign exchange headwinds reduced reported revenue by approximately 1.8 percentage points.
The company is party to ongoing litigation regarding retail worker overtime disputes.
Two customers accounted for 29% of total revenue.

Outlook
For the fourth quarter, the company expects revenue between $5.80 billion and $6.00 billion and operating margin of approximately 5.8%.
Management noted macroeconomic headwinds and cautious consumer sentiment entering the quarter.
